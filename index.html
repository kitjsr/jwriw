About Us

JWR Industrial Works began operations in 2015 as a sister unit of Tatanagar Wire Ropes Pvt. Ltd. (Tatanagar Wire Ropes Pvt. Ltd. was established in 1987) In 2024 all operations and experienced personnel moved to JWR IW. The plant is located at M24P Phase 4 Adityapur Industrial Area, Gamharia, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand 832108.

We combine modernized rope‑making machinery with a calibrated laboratory and experienced management to deliver certified rope solutions for Coal Mining applications.
